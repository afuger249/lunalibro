# release Notes - Lunalibro v0.8.0

## 📖 Storytelling Engine (v2.0)
- **Advanced Narrative Logic**: Rewrote the AI "Author" prompt to focus on professional storytelling arcs (Setup, Conflict, Resolution).
- **Richer Content**: Increased text volume per page (3-5 sentences) with a focus on joyful and captivating sensory language.
- **Magical Illustrations**: Updated image generation prompts to prioritize atmospheric lighting, emotional expressions, and whimsical details.
- **Character Fidelity**: Improved visual description injection to ensure characters (especially Sea Turtles and Hamsters) look consistent across all generations.

## 🎙️ Chat & Learning Features
- **Push-to-Talk (PTT)**: Replaced auto-listening with a manual microphone toggle to prevent AI interruptions and give users full control over their Spanish speaking time.
- **Smart TTS Filtering**: Implemented a global emoji filter for Text-to-Speech. Emojis remain visible but are no longer spoken aloud, ensuring a natural conversation flow.
- **Difficulty Lock (A0/A1)**: Radically simplified the AI response logic for young beginners, strictly limiting vocabulary and grammar complexity for the earliest learners.

## 🎨 UI & Character Customization
- **Sea Turtle Update**: Rebranded the "Turtle" character to "Sea Turtle" with updated color palettes (Teal, Ocean Blue) and aquatic-themed accessories.
- **Selected State Fix**: Resolved a CSS shorthand conflict that caused selected customization buttons to appear empty/invisible.
- **Premium Branding**: Integrated new 3D assets for Word Rush game modes and updated the Luna & Friends logo throughout the app.

## 🌍 Reader & Bilingual Support
- **Full Bilingual Sync**: Confirmed perfect synchronization of English/Spanish toggles across the entire StoryReader experience.
- **Audio Swapping**: Instant voice profile swapping (Male/Female/English/Spanish) based on user toggle.
